FINAL UPDATE GUIDE 2
Chapter II: Review of Related Literature and Studies
Pages 13-18

Purpose
This guide focuses on the exact section that starts on Page 13 and continues until Page 18 of Chapter II. The wording in these pages should match the actual Laravel pharmacy system and should not exceed what the implemented code can support.

======================================================================
FIRST: LIST OF WHAT NEEDS TO CHANGE
======================================================================

1. Page 13-14: revise the local studies discussion so it does not sound like a verified finding of the study. It should describe possible pharmacy conditions, not proven local losses.

2. Page 13-14: replace absolute statements about expired stock, financial losses, and local system conditions with careful wording such as "may contribute" and "should be supported by local evidence."

3. Page 15: qualify statements about customer service and consultation time. The system supports prescription records and verification workflow, but it does not replace pharmacist judgment.

4. Page 15-17: keep the synthesis grounded in the code: inventory, purchasing, sales, stock movement, batch tracking, barcode lookup, POS workflow, low-stock monitoring, reorder suggestions, audit logs, and internal compliance exception reports.

5. Page 15-18: remove claims that the system guarantees regulatory compliance, establishes an immutable audit trail, or meets international standards unless this is proven through separate formal evaluation.

6. Page 13-18: use wording like "may support," "supports internal review," and "transaction-triggered updates" instead of absolute phrases like "prevents," "ensures," and "guarantees."
