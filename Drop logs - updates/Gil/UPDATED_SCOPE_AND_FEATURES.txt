E. SCOPE AND DELIMITATION (UPDATED)
================================================================================

Scope

The study focused on the development of a web-based pharmacy inventory
management system intended for small and medium-sized pharmacies. The system
included the following features:

a. User login, authentication, and role-based access control
b. Medicine inventory management, including product, category, and supplier records
c. Stock monitoring with automatic updates from purchases and sales
d. Batch and lot number tracking
e. Expiry date tracking and expired-product monitoring
f. Low-stock and near-expiry alerts
g. Barcode generation and barcode-based product lookup using compatible scanners
h. Point-of-sale and sales transaction management
i. Prescription submission and verification
j. Supplier management and purchase monitoring
k. Stock movement, audit trail, and regulatory compliance records
l. Inventory, sales, purchase, supplier, valuation, and expiry report generation
m. Automated reorder suggestions and short-term medicine demand forecasting
n. Mobile-responsive inventory checking
o. Temperature reading and storage-condition monitoring
p. Administrative dashboard for monitoring overall inventory status

The system is accessible through computers and mobile devices with an internet
or local network connection. Barcode scanning requires a compatible barcode
scanner or camera-enabled device.

Delimitations

These are the limitations of the study:

a. The system is intended for pharmacy inventory, purchasing, sales, and
   dispensing management. It does not include a full accounting or general
   ledger module.
b. Online payment processing, customer-facing e-commerce, and online ordering
   features are not included.
c. The system depends on internet or local network connectivity and server
   availability for access and real-time synchronization.
d. Barcode scanning depends on compatible external hardware or a camera-enabled
   device; the system does not provide barcode-scanner hardware.
e. The system is designed for authorized pharmacy users, such as administrators,
   pharmacists, and staff.
f. The baseline system is intended for a single pharmacy and does not include
   multi-branch operations or third-party supplier, insurance, or billing-system
   integrations.

REPORT GENERATION CAPABILITY:
================================================================================

The system now includes 10 comprehensive report types (previously not 
detailed in Chapter 1):

1. Current Stock Inventory Report
   - Real-time inventory status
   - Product quantities and valuations
   - Supplier information
   - Cost and selling price analysis

2. Low Stock Alert Report
   - Products below reorder level
   - Shortage calculations
   - Reorder recommendations

3. Expiring and Expired Medicines Report
   - Expiration date tracking
   - Critical expiry notifications (≤30 days)
   - Batch-specific tracking
   - Shelf-life remaining calculations

4. Stock Movement Report
   - Complete stock activity history
   - Movement type categorization
   - Running balance calculations
   - Date-filtered views

5. Purchase Order Report
   - Supplier purchase history
   - Order status tracking
   - Delivery date monitoring
   - Order value analysis

6. Supplier Performance Report
   - Purchase frequency analysis
   - On-time delivery metrics
   - Units received tracking
   - Reliability scoring

7. Sales and Dispensing Report
   - Transaction history
   - Cashier/staff performance
   - Revenue analysis
   - Customer tracking

8. Inventory Valuation Report
   - Current stock value calculations
   - Cost-basis valuations
   - Selling price valuations
   - Financial reporting support

9. Batch and Lot Tracking Report
   - Complete batch traceability
   - Manufacturing/expiry dates
   - Batch movement history
   - Compliance documentation

10. Regulatory Compliance Report
    - Missing batch numbers
    - Missing barcode identification
    - Expired stock alerts
    - Compliance gap analysis
    - Corrective action recommendations
